    How the weighted rating works: each book's average is blended with the overall average (4.05) as if it had extra "average" votes. Books with few ratings get pulled toward the middle; books with many keep their real average. Formula: (n·r + m·C) ÷ (n + m), where m is the weighting strength.

    Data: ~46,000 books from the Goodreads "Best Books Ever" list, scraped in 2020 (Casanova Lozano & Costa Planells, CC BY-NC 4.0). Ratings are from that snapshot, so newer books and recent rating changes aren't included. StoryGraph doesn't publish its data, so each book links to a StoryGraph search instead.
